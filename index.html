From Sketchbook to Store

Hi, I'm Antreya. I'm an illustrator with a huge obsession for turning everyday things into cute, colorful, and cartoon-filled creations. What started as doodles in the corners of notebooks slowly became a world of characters, ideas, and tiny moments of joy. I created Antreyart Store to share those moments with others. Every design is hand-drawn with care, creativity, and heart. Whether it's a planner, memo pad, greeting card, journal, or future creation, every product is designed to make everyday life a little brighter. Because art doesn't belong only in galleries. It belongs on your desk, in your notebook, in your plans, and in your daily routine.
